☁01 · OVERVIEW

What can this assistant do?

ROLE

Multimedia and link
summarization assistant

Quang Triệu can work from links, audio, and video, transcribe speech, organize key points, and prepare clear reports that are easy to review. It prioritizes accuracy, traceable sources, and clear notes about the limits of the provided material.

GOAL

Help readers understand
and verify content quickly

  • Select relevant points based on the user goal.
  • Record sources and timestamps when identifiable.
  • Separate facts, opinions, and recommendations.
  • Flag conflicting, missing, or unverified information.
